# Changelog

## v1.0.7 — 2026-10-18

- Share the same six quota palettes across capsule, white ring, and aurora ring.
- Tint ring shading, highlights, glow, and endpoint markers to match each quota stage.
- Export an offline all-skin color comparison without changing saved window positions.
- Reduce capsule height by 25 percent while preserving width, bold text, and the uniform five-pixel rim.

## v1.0.6 — 2026-10-18

- Make the highest capsule quota stage greener while retaining the blue 80 percent stage.

## v1.0.5 — 2026-10-18

- Use six capsule color stages at 80, 60, 40, 20, and 10 percent remaining.
- Add teal and gold stages, preserving the existing blue, purple, low-quota orange, and critical red palettes.

## v1.0.4 — 2026-10-18

- Enlarge the capsule percentage from 27 to 34 and use bold numerals.
- Smooth the dark-to-light color progression with a continuous color curve.

## v1.0.3 — 2026-10-18

- Make every capsule quota color progress from a dark left edge to a lighter right frontier.
- Remove the pale strip at the left and keep frontier haze away from it at low quota levels.

## v1.0.2 — 2026-10-18

- Replace perimeter scanning with a red glow across the entire widget for task completion.
- Keep the three-second lifetime, rounded clipping, and alert continuity during skin changes.
- Add offline render exports for nine capsule quota levels and completion-glow animation frames.

## v1.0.1 — 2026-10-18

- Distribute the capsule's glowing particles across the entire remaining-quota fill.
- Keep particles clipped to the animated fill, with gentle drift and twinkle.

## v1.0.0 — 2026-10-18

- Initial public release under the MIT license.
- Three context-menu skins: animated capsule, white ring, and aurora ring.
- 130×130 ring layouts with quota percentages and reset countdowns.
- Three-second red perimeter sweep for successful local task completion.
- Single-window skin switching, tray support, saved appearance and position.
- Windows build script, 14 offline self-tests, and GitHub Actions build.
